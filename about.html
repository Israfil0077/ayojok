Who we are

Ayojok brings everything you need for a wedding or event into one place: trusted vendors, a clear budget and personal consultants. Couples can compare photographers, venues, caterers, decorators and more, book them online and follow every detail from a single dashboard.

Our goal is simple: less stress for families, more time to enjoy the celebration.

About this project

This website is a front-end demo built from the AYOJOK 2.0 Figma design, created for the System Analysis and Design course at United International University. The design won 3rd Runner-Up at the UIU CSE Project Show, Summer 2025. Vendors, bookings and accounts on this site are sample data; nothing is stored.
